Your documents never leave your phone.

ScaniPro scans paper into numbered exhibits, fingerprints every page with SHA-256 at the moment of capture, and keeps an append-only custody log, so you can show later that a document is exactly what you captured.

Sealed at capture

Each page image is hashed the instant you finish scanning. Originals are never modified.

Custody log

Every action is logged with timestamp, device, iOS and app version, and the file's hash. Entries are hash-chained.

Searchable PDFs

Text is recognised on the device and embedded invisibly, so every exhibit is searchable offline.

Evidence packets

Cover page, exhibit index, stamped exhibits and a hash manifest, plus CSV and JSON manifests.

No account. No cloud. No analytics. No tracking.
Scanning, OCR, hashing and PDF creation run entirely on your iPhone. Files leave only when you share them. Read the privacy policy.

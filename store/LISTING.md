## Description
CaseSeal is a private, evidence-grade PDF scanner for iPhone. Scan documents, receipts and records into tamper-evident exhibits: every page is fingerprinted with SHA-256, timestamped and written to a chain-of-custody log, so you can show later that a document is exactly what you captured. Built for legal cases, insurance claims, HR investigations and personal disputes.

YOUR DOCUMENTS NEVER LEAVE YOUR PHONE
Scanning, text recognition, hashing and PDF creation all run on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no cloud sync, no analytics and no tracking. Files leave the app only when you choose to share them.

BUILT FOR
• Paralegals and small law firms assembling exhibits
• Investigators and HR teams documenting a matter
• Insurance adjusters collecting claim paperwork
• Anyone gathering records for a dispute, claim or hearing

CASES AND EXHIBITS
• Organise documents into cases with a title, reference number and matter date
• Each scan is saved as an exhibit and numbered automatically
• Numbers are never reused, so gaps are always explained

SEALED AT CAPTURE
• Every page image is hashed with SHA-256 the moment you finish scanning
• An exhibit-level capture digest locks the pages and their order
• Originals are never modified

CUSTODY LOG
• Every action is recorded: captured, text recognised, PDF generated, renamed, annotated, signed, verified, exported and shared
• Each entry stores an ISO 8601 timestamp, device model, iOS version, app version and the SHA-256 of the file after the action
• Entries are hash-chained and append-only; any edit, removal or reordering is detected
• Verify Integrity re-hashes every file and re-checks the chain whenever you like

SCAN, IMPORT AND TOOLS
• Document, book (two-page spreads split automatically) and text-only scanning
• QR code reader
• Import photos and files; imported PDFs are kept byte-for-byte and hashed
• Export any exhibit as text, images, .docx or .pptx (Pro), or merge exhibits into one PDF (Pro)
• Expense reports: totals read from scanned receipts, exported as PDF and CSV (Pro)
• Recently Deleted keeps withdrawn exhibits for 30 days

SEARCHABLE PDFS WITH ON-DEVICE OCR
• Text is recognised on your iPhone and placed as an invisible layer in each exhibit PDF
• Search every word across every case, even in airplane mode
• Copy recognised text in one tap

EVIDENCE PACKETS (PRO)
• One PDF with a cover page, exhibit index, stamped exhibit headers and footers, and a hash manifest page
• The exact exhibit files can be embedded so recipients can re-hash them with standard tools
• Hash manifests exported as CSV and JSON, plus a ZIP bundle with everything

SIGNATURES AND ANNOTATIONS (PRO)
• Sign, draw and add notes on any exhibit
• Changes are saved as a new version with its own SHA-256, linked to the version it came from
• The original capture stays untouched

PRIVATE AND LOCKED
• Face ID or passcode lock, with content hidden in the app switcher
• Files are stored with iOS Complete data protection

FREE AND PRO
The free version includes 1 case and 5 exhibits with scanning, OCR, search, hashing, custody logs and integrity verification. A one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited cases and exhibits, evidence packet export, signatures and annotations, .docx/.pptx export, merging and expense reports. No subscription.

CaseSeal produces records that support authenticity. It is not legal advice, and admissibility is decided by the rules of your jurisdiction.
